# Bundled fallback haikus for haiku.py
# One haiku per block, separated by blank lines, three lines each (5-7-5).
# Lines starting with # are comments. Every entry must pass haiku.validate().

Pale moon over pines
silver light on still water
a small frog jumps once

Rain on the roof top
each drop a tiny drumbeat
spring is waking up

Autumn leaves falling
the old road remembers rain
footprints fill with gold

Winter morning light
breath becomes a small white cloud
the street sleeps in snow

Quiet computer
a cursor blinks in the dark
code grows like a vine

Lights across the field
small lanterns above the grass
summer holds its breath

Ocean tide returns
dragging shells along the shore
the moon pulls the night

Sip of hot tea now
steam writes a word then is gone
a slow morning starts

Two crows on a wire
counting cars beneath them pass
grey afternoon sky

Cherry blossoms drift
down the alley and away
spring does not look back

Bells ring in the town
the sound crosses open fields
birds leave the wires

Deep night, the laptop
one more bug then off to sleep
the fan hums softly

Sunlight through the blinds
stripes of gold across the floor
dust dancing slowly

Old cat by the door
watching rain she cannot reach
she dreams of warm stone

Thunder far away
the sky clears its throat again
green smell of wet earth

Train through the valley
carrying them home at night
windows glow like stars

Paper on the desk
blank page waiting for a word
and the pen thinks first

Snow covers the roofs
chimneys breathe a thin gray line
the town is dreaming

Half moon like a nail
hammering the dark to stay
stars line up behind

Cicadas sing loud
heat bends the afternoon air
no one moves at all

River over stones
the water keeps talking on
none of us stop here

Library quiet
pages turning like small waves
the sea under glass

Bike light in the dark
a moving star along the road
and home is close now

Fog swallows the hills
the world grows small and quiet
one lone black pine tree

Late train, empty car
my reflection rides along
asleep by the glass

Clouds over the peak
the mountain wears a white scarf
winter comes early

Spiders in the grass
thin silk strung between the green
dew holds the morning

Coffee going cold
the meeting runs on and on
the window turns gray

Swallows leave the eaves
the nest waits under the roof
spring returns again

Wind through the wires
power lines hum a low note
and the dark keeps time

Puddle in the road
the whole sky fits in one step
clouds under my shoe

Old radio on
voices from a summer past
dust in the speaker

Ants cross the kitchen
a tiny parade of black
the crumbs lead them home

Sleeping city lights
the window keeps a square of night
I close my own eyes

Bamboo after rain
every joint remembers how
to bend and stand up

Snowflake on my sleeve
it fades out before I blink
winter signs in ink

Bus stop in the rain
everyone shares one small roof
strangers stand closer

Mango tree at noon
fruit falls and the grass shakes once
bees go back to sleep

Lamps along the ward
machines breathe in even time
morning waits outside

Bicycle bell rings
the sound skips across the lane
geese lift off slowly
