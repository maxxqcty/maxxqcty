# Bundled haiku pool for haiku.py -- one per day of a month, 31 in all.
# One haiku per block, separated by blank lines, three lines each (5-7-5).

# Register: Shakespearean -- time, mortality, ambition, vanity, the stage.

O fair Rose, thou art
too brief a flower for me
and summer must die

Doth dust remember
the dust that was a crowned king
no tongue shall reply

We are but players
who act our parts in shadow
then fall the curtain

The good year grows old
and swallows build against eaves
while summer still holds

O look in the glass
the face that looks is not thine
but a mask of time

Now white winter comes
the black tree writes on the snow
and no man shall read

Anon the long sleep
no drum shall wake the still house
not one may follow

O crown, thou art cold
and heavy with the dead kings
who first did wear it

The dead do not walk
yet still I hear them at night
calling out my name

Beauty is a rose
that knows the frost ere it falls
and bows its fair head

And dust shall be king
when gold and marble are gone
and the throne is clay

How lone the night is
where owls repeat to the stones
the names of dead kings

Spin the long dark thread
no sword may cut what ye spin
nor stay the bright loom

A good name is wind
it passes through the loud town
and then is no more

Old Time hath no shore
yet ev'ry wave returns sand
and all vanishes

The fire speaks low
to those who sit with the dead
in winter's dark rooms

I know not my heart
it beats beneath a false breast
like some dark traitor

And we are small ships
on seas that no man has mapped
and then the night comes

Water keeps no word
it gives back the voice you lend
then lets the sound go

We all turn to dust
kings, harlots, jesters, and all
and one grave for all

Now the green comes back
where yesteryear lay in ice
and one small thrush sings

What doth a crown cost
go ask the fields of the slain
the dead still cry out

This ink is black blood
drawn from a heart that says no
and it shall not die

Now the clock strikes one
and the long hall holds its breath
and then sleeps again

Love is but a torch
that burns the hand that holds it
and will not be held

Who watches the night
when watchmen sleep at the gate
and the moon walks round

Far off a bell rings
for one who will not come back
and still no one knows

The patient earth keeps
all our small bones and our names
and never asks more

Speak, and then be still
the whole wide world turns to hush
and waits until dawn

Yet we pull no thread
the dance goes on without us
and we dance again

And so the day ends
the candle gutters to ash
and the morn comes on
